FAQ

Questions creators ask

Still unsure? Visit the Help Center or contact us.

Is EliteURL free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, shortening links and withdrawing your earnings are free. There is no withdrawal fee. You only need a valid email address to sign up.

How much can I earn?

Your income is your verified views multiplied by the rate for each visitor’s country, so it depends entirely on your audience. Traffic from higher-paying countries earns more than traffic from lower-paying ones. The live rate card on this page shows what is paid per 1,000 verified views. We never promise a fixed income.

When can I withdraw my earnings?

New earnings first appear as Estimated and move to Confirmed after review. Only your Confirmed balance can be withdrawn, once it reaches the minimum payout shown in your dashboard. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and paid within the payout window shown on the rate card.

Which payment methods do you support?

You can withdraw to UPI, PayPal or a bank account. Every completed payout carries a transaction reference that you can see in your dashboard.

Why do rates differ by country?

Advertisers pay different amounts depending on where a visitor is located. That is why traffic from some countries earns several times more than others. Rates are set per country and shown openly on the rate card.

Do my visitors see advertising?

Yes. Before reaching the destination, visitors complete a few short steps that include advertising. That advertising is what pays for creator earnings. If you prefer, you can also create password-protected or expiring links.

What kind of traffic is not allowed?

Automated or incentivised traffic, clicking your own links, proxies and VPNs, and links to malware, phishing, adult content, gambling or pirated material. Such views are not counted and repeat violations can lead to account suspension.

Can I protect or schedule my links?

Yes. From your dashboard you can add a custom alias, protect a link with a password, set an expiry date and generate a QR code for any short link.
