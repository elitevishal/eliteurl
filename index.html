FAQ

Questions creators ask

How much can I earn?

Your income is your verified views multiplied by the rate for that visitor's country, and creators keep almost all of it. Indian traffic typically earns a fraction of US traffic. There is no fixed figure and no guarantee.

When can I withdraw?

Once your confirmed balance crosses $5. Earnings stay in an estimated state during the day and move to confirmed automatically every night at 12:00 AM IST.

Which payment methods do you support?

PayPal, UPI and direct bank transfer. Every payout carries a transaction reference you can verify.

What traffic is not allowed?

Automated or incentivised traffic, self-clicks, proxies and VPNs, and links to malware, phishing, adult content, gambling or pirated material. Violations result in suspension and forfeiture of unpaid balances.

Do you show ads to my audience?

Yes. Visitors see a short sponsored step before reaching the destination, with a visible countdown and a clearly labelled continue button. This is what generates the revenue you earn from.
